# Gameplay screenshots for Minepedia

Rules (per Mojang Usage Guidelines, minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines):
- ONLY screenshots taken by David of his own gameplay, or community screenshots with a
  verified permissive license / documented permission (record source + license below).
- NO official Mojang art, renders, logos, brand-kit assets, or marketing images.
- Serve locally from this folder. No hotlinking.
- Every image used on the site must have an entry in src/data/screenshots.json with:
  file, alt, caption, section, source ("david" or URL), license note.

Naming: <section>-<topic>-<nn>.webp (convert to webp, max ~1600px wide).
